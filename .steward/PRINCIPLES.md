# PRINCIPLES

1. **Silly idea, serious method.** The refusal to stop measuring is the joke.
2. **CLI first forever.** A capability is complete headlessly before TUI or
   native presentation.
3. **One event, one truth.** Physics, proof, story, audio, visuals, haptics, and
   consequences cite one authoritative provenance graph.
4. **No assumed source.** Human, biological, terrestrial, three-dimensional, and
   familiar-universe behavior are profiles, not the ontology.
5. **Labels are earned.** Regimes cite calculated state and versioned policy.
6. **Conservation of Ass.** Transfers use explicit control volumes and
   double-entry ledgers.
7. **Translation admits loss.** Strict, approximate, and comic mappings report
   incompatibility and residuals instead of fabricating equivalence.
8. **The story never cheats.** Narrative reacts to immutable simulation facts and
   never rerolls an outcome for drama.
9. **Ordinary remains important.** Precision, culture, timing, and proof can make
   a pfft more rewarding than an explosion.
10. **Proof has levels.** Replay, internal consistency, verification, validation,
    and fictional-law consistency are separate claims.
11. **Polish is functional.** Accessibility, errors, pipes, cancellation,
    portability, archives, packaging, and recovery are product features.
12. **Quality stays measurable.** Formatting and lint are clean, tests pass, core
    coverage remains at least 80 percent, and CI is green.
