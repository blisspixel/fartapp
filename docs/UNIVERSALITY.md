# Bounded universality contract

F.A.R.T. Lab uses “universal” as a product aspiration and a protocol design
constraint. It does not claim one ontology, equation set, language, sense,
humor system, or metaphysics applies everywhere. The scientific core is a
bounded software instrument. It can encode only cases that can be identified,
scoped, versioned, and related through declared machine-readable contracts.

“Fart” is the intentionally ridiculous presentation umbrella. It is not a
required scientific category. A selected classifier may map supported claims
to an emission analogue, including a release, transfer, relaxation, boundary
crossing, or another locally defined relation. Exact refusal to make that
mapping is a successful result.

## The outer report precedes the case

A stateless `OperationReport` or validation and refusal envelope may exist before
the Lab can accept a case. Its minimum is report schema, stage status, consulted
inputs, and typed diagnostics or refusal. Law references, scope, requested
capabilities, and other resolved fields appear only after the corresponding
stage succeeds.

The current scenario probe is such an envelope. It can reject malformed input,
an unsupported schema, an unresolved law, or an unknown capability without
allocating a record identity. `outside_representable_ontology` can likewise end
at this boundary. A failed attempt to represent something does not create a
fictional in-ontology case merely so the failure has somewhere to live.

The minimal opaque unresolved-capability fixture makes the capability-resolution
boundary executable. A syntax-valid, schema-valid input with an exact law
reference can still end without a capability result, case, ambient default, or
solver. Its case-operation axes report selection `not-declared` and admission
and execution `not-applicable`. The `capability_not_defined` result is
name-resolution evidence only, not a law, policy, ontology, or case-operation
admission, execution, or refusal result.

## The committed-case kernel belongs to the Lab

Once an operation is admitted and commits a Lab case record, its minimum record
contains four software-level objects:

1. `RecordIdentity`, which identifies one Lab capture, computation, comparison,
   or other committed case. It says nothing about identity,
   recurrence, or time in the represented context.
2. `LawContextSet`, which selects versioned rule systems for explicit parts of
   the case. A singleton is ordinary. Multiple contexts require declared scope
   assignments and bridge rules.
3. `Scope`, which is an opaque addressable application boundary. It need not be
   spatial, temporal, material, localized, connected, or object-shaped.
4. `ProvenanceGraph`, which records how the Lab obtained and transformed its
   own claims. Its edges are software evidence relations unless a law context
   separately licenses a source-law interpretation.

These objects describe an accepted instrument record, not the furniture of every
possible reality. Even “graph,” “record,” and “identity” name finite software
representations. A purported case that cannot enter this representation is
reported in the outer envelope as `outside_representable_ontology`.

`Occurrence`, `Realization`, `Participant`, `Object`, `State`, `Event`,
`Observer`, `Cause`, and `Signal` are optional context capabilities. They are
not aliases for the minimum record. `DischargeEvent` is an Earth-continuum
specialization. It is never the superclass of existence.

## Nothing familiar arrives implicitly

Unless the law context applicable to a scoped part defines a concept and the
accepted case supplies every required binding, the core does not assume:

- a human, organism, body, species, anatomy, emitter, aperture, or exterior;
- Earth, a planet, a room, an atmosphere, a gravitational field, or SI units;
- matter, gas, mass, energy, pressure, temperature, sound, or conservation;
- space, dimensionality, coordinates, distance, geometry, topology, or
  locality;
- linear, branching, cyclic, discrete, continuous, or even defined source-law
  time;
- objects, persistent identity, state, events, causes, interventions, or
  recurrence;
- observers, agents, individual minds, societies, privacy, intention, or
  responsibility;
- symbols, propositions, language, compositional meaning, audible channels, or
  shared humor;
- deterministic evolution, probability, randomness, computability, decidable
  comparison, or a unique solution.

Absence is not encoded as a zero, empty Earth panel, anonymous person, silent
audio track, Cartesian placeholder, or default English description. The
applicable capability is omitted or returns a typed result such as
`not_applicable`, `not_declared`, `unavailable`, `undecidable_within_budget`, or
`outside_representable_ontology`.

## Law-scoped meaning

A law context declares only the structural modules it supports. Candidate
modules include relations, ordering, state, identity, adjacency, topology,
metric, locality, fields, quantities, dimensions, units, transition rules,
equations, symmetries, invariants, conserved currents, observations,
interventions, and representations. This list is extensible and does not imply
that the concepts are mutually independent or meaningful in every context.

The current Go report keeps eight useful fields distinct: law definition,
implementation, closure, applicability, evidence, trust, backend feasibility,
and resource feasibility. They do not form one universal assessment algebra.
They are an alpha projection across future catalog, provider, optional
formal-system, case or operation, claim-evidence, authorization,
execution-planning, and resource-admission contracts.

The smallest shared evaluation candidate distinguishes only `evaluated`, which
requires one outcome owned by the selected profile, from `not_evaluated`, which
forbids an outcome. Generic `unknown` and `undetermined` are not shared process
states. Case, measurement, operation, backend, policy, and budget bindings may
be absent or unevaluated and belong to enclosing contracts when applicable.

A finite catalog snapshot can close membership only over the exact set stored
inside that Lab artifact. `not_registered` then means only that this set contains
no exact binding match. Product Catalog Lookup Closure must additionally bind an
exact catalog-scope revision, resolved declaration-authority reference, and
declaration-attribution scope. Neither form is ontological completeness. An
authority reference is an attribution key and need not identify a person,
institution, agent, owner, lawgiver, organism, location, or universe.

Exact authority-reference matching is a Lab relation between one opaque
scope-plus-authority binding and record entries in a retained finite
snapshot. Zero, one, or multiple matches describe only that artifact. They do
not require or infer an entity, person, institution, organism, location,
source-law occurrence, time, network identity, or semantic authority. Record
order and revision carry no chronology, priority, or fallback meaning.

Snapshot-relative authority resolution is also a Lab software relation. Its
zero, one, and multiple-match outcomes describe only whether this candidate rule
can select exactly one record entry from the retained finite artifact. Even a
positive witness asserts nothing about the nature, embodiment, agency,
personhood, species, anatomy, location, geometry, dimensionality, unit system,
language, universe, network identity, temporal status, legitimacy, trust, or
external existence of anything named by the opaque reference. Multiple entries
are not evidence of multiple beings, authorities, identities, or meanings.

Exact registration-authority binding is a Lab software relation among one
retained snapshot-resolved authority witness, one positive structural
registration, and one closed internal comparison-rule identity. A match says
only that two opaque catalog-scope references and two opaque
declaration-authority references are pairwise equal under that rule. It does not
identify a creator, owner, speaker, lawgiver, institution, organism, entity,
location, universe, or source. A no-match does not establish external
difference, unrelatedness, nonexistence, illegitimacy, ambiguity, attribution,
or registration absence. Rule identity and revision are Lab identity material,
not natural language, universal meaning, provenance, or source-law time.

No current field may be collapsed into or inferred solely from another. A
fictional axiom can be implemented and formally checked without being
empirically validated. A real-world model can be defined yet unavailable on a
selected backend. A document can be valid while no operation was requested.
Realization is only one optional operation kind. The decomposition gate is in
[CAPABILITY_REPORT.md](CAPABILITY_REPORT.md).

## Identity and impermanence

The Lab always distinguishes its record identity from any identity relation in
a represented context. A new capture or computation receives a fresh record
identity. That product rule does not claim that the represented case recurred,
failed to recur, happened in time, or was an event at all.

Replay presents retained evidence. Reconstruction repeats a declared Lab
procedure. Re-enactment is available only where applicable law contexts support
the term. None of these operations can assert source-law equivalence without an
explicit relation licensed by the selected contexts and, when necessary, an
inter-law bridge.

This preserves the project's “every fart is a snowflake” idea without turning
impermanence into a universal metaphysical claim. The passing human experience
of a presentation and the identity rules of a represented context remain
separate.

## Translation is a family of operations

The Universal Flatulence Translator never treats all communication as human
language. It distinguishes:

- semantic translation under a declared shared semantic basis;
- structural mapping that preserves selected relations or invariants;
- signal transcoding under declared channel constraints;
- experience analogy as an explicitly artistic or comic projection.

A structural match is not silently upgraded to shared meaning. A sonification
does not prove that the source produced sound. An English joke does not prove
that the represented participants recognize humor. The target may receive a
precise no-mapping result, including separate reasons for incompatibility,
missing observables, insufficient channels, ambiguity, information loss,
privacy, policy refusal, undecidability, and bounded-ontology failure.

## Retained-case surface contract

CLI, TUI, native, MCP, A2A, archives, and exported artifacts consume the same
capability report once a case has been admitted and retained. Their future
retained-case minimum is deliberately austere:

- record identity and schema revision;
- selected law contexts and scope bindings;
- requested capability results and exact refusals;
- retained Lab claims, evidence status, uncertainty where defined, and
  provenance;
- view and presentation transformations plus declared loss.

The current v0.7 scenario probe is earlier than this contract. It validates
document structure and capability references only, requests no operation, and
creates no record identity, case result, archive, or provenance graph.

Every other pane is conditional. A relation-only case does not receive a fake
timeline. A nonspatial case does not receive an empty three-dimensional room.
A context without acoustics does not receive “0 dB.” A context without human
language does not receive an invisible English gloss. The native app may render
a compatible human-spatial analogy, but it must label the projection and its
losses.

## Negative-space conformance matrix

Universality is tested by removing assumptions, not by adding increasingly
fantastical nouns. Before schemas or interfaces freeze, the project must ship
tiny fixtures for these cases:

| Fixture | Concepts intentionally absent | Required result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Minimal opaque | Localized human-language presentation and every optional structural module | JSON catalog inspection and scenario-schema validation succeed using locale-invariant engineering tokens defined only by the Lab protocol |
| Atemporal relation | Time, state, geometry, units, source, and observer | Relation capability resolves without fabricated fields |
| Nonspatial transfer | Geometry, localized emitter, gas, and Earth quantities | Only the declared transfer relation appears |
| Partial order | A global clock and total event order | Views preserve incomparability rather than sorting it away |
| Cyclic structure | A privileged beginning, end, or recurrence interpretation | The cycle is retained without inventing a timeline |
| Nonsymbolic observation | Human language, propositions, and audible communication | A bounded structural or signal mapping works without prose semantics |
| Measurement back-action | Passive observation | Accepted interaction changes the authoritative case result and identity |
| No bridge | Cross-context compatibility | Coupling is refused with `inter_law_bridge_missing` |
| Explicit bridge | Implicit shared units, ordering, identity, or conservation | Only the bridge's declared relations cross the boundary |
| No shared observable | A common comparison coordinate | Translation returns `no_shared_observable` |
| Unrepresentable | Finite identification or encoding | The Lab returns `outside_representable_ontology` without simulating |
| Lossy spatial projection | Native source geometry matching the display | The artifact records embedding, distortion, and information loss |

Those tokens are finite machine symbols, not natural language,
language-neutral notation, or universally shared meaning. The minimal opaque
fixture proves only that this exact application-level capability request does
not require localized presentation or a declared optional structural module. It
does not claim that the represented reality lacks structure.

The current multi-law probe-limit fixture is earlier than the `No bridge` row.
It proves only that the one-context provisional schema will not interpret,
choose among, or merge multiple context entries. Because that schema has no bridge
representation, `multi_law_not_supported` cannot imply incompatibility or
`inter_law_bridge_missing`.

Passing one fixture does not establish real-world universality. Together they
prevent ordinary Earth discharge, human narrative, and three-dimensional UI
assumptions from hardening into the core by accident.

## Standard for new work

Every new schema field, command, pane, story beat, agent observation, and
scientific metric must answer four questions:

1. Which law context or Lab-level contract owns this concept?
2. What happens when that concept is not defined?
3. Which executable negative-space fixture proves the fallback?
4. Does the presentation claim more meaning than the retained evidence?

If those answers are missing, the feature remains a design candidate. The
project can be absurdly ambitious without pretending that familiar physics or
human interpretation is the center of everything.
