# Candidate visual identity

F.A.R.T. Lab needs a mark credible on a scientific instrument and funny on a
shirt without becoming novelty-shop clip art. The identity uses one stable
institutional mark and an unlimited family of event artifacts.

The current candidate is **The Open Isobar**. It is not a cleared or final
trademark.

![Open Isobar candidate mark with a broken pressure contour and one asymmetric wavefront](../brand/source/open-isobar-candidate.svg)

## The Open Isobar

The mark is a heavy pressure contour interrupted by one calibrated opening. One
asymmetric wavefront crosses the opening and continues beyond the boundary. The
source is not depicted. The boundary never quite closes because the encounter
has passed.

The mark means pressure, boundary, emission, measurement, and impermanence. It
does not mean a specific body, species, medium, world, or geometry.

Prohibited core motifs include:

- Anatomy, faces, buttocks, or a literal source.
- Brown clouds, odor squiggles, hazard stripes, or gross-out clip art.
- Government, military, university, or real standards-body imitation.
- A particle swarm assembling giant typography.
- A familiar cinema-ident pitch rise, chord, cadence, or reveal.
- Decorative red. Red belongs to failed verification and unsafe state.

## Construction candidate

The vector master uses a 1024-unit square:

- Center at `(512, 512)`.
- Nominal contour radius `288`.
- Nominal contour stroke `88`.
- Opening of approximately 52 degrees centered on the positive horizontal axis.
- Flat terminal cuts perpendicular to the contour.
- One asymmetric wavefront that begins inside, crosses the opening, and decays
  outside.
- No text inside the symbol.
- Clear space of at least one contour-stroke width.

The exact geometry remains subject to optical drawing, recognition tests,
confusion review, and trademark clearance. Small variants are redrawn, not
blindly scaled:

| Size | Variant |
| --- | --- |
| 64 px and above | Full contour and wavefront |
| 24 to 63 px | Enlarged opening and shortened wavefront |
| 16 to 23 px | Broken contour and one terminal stroke |
| One bit | Solid ink with no color or transparency dependency |
| Embroidery | At least 1.2 mm stroke and 1.8 mm opening after sampling |

## Identity hierarchy

1. **Open Isobar:** the instrument, application, repository, and stable product
   identity.
2. **Conservation Triad:** circle for mass, directional chevron for momentum,
   and diamond for energy. It is notation, not the app icon.
3. **Plumeprint:** a two-dimensional artifact belonging to one observed event.
4. **Fartflake:** a three-dimensional artifact belonging to one observed event.
5. **Unofficial Field Observation:** a deliberately distinct community badge
   for remixes, fan work, and unverified artifacts.

The stable mark identifies the instrument. A generated artifact identifies an
event. A Fartflake never becomes the app icon, release seal, or repository
avatar, no matter how beautiful it is.

## Lockups

- **Primary:** Open Isobar, `F.A.R.T. LAB`, and the full expansion.
- **Scientific:** full expansion dominant, with `UNIVERSAL EMISSION INSTRUMENT`
  as descriptor.
- **Compact:** Open Isobar plus `F.A.R.T. LAB`, only after name clearance.
- **Instrument seal:** mark inside a typographic ring with
  `MASS / MOMENTUM / ENERGY`. It cannot imply public authority or certification.
- **CLI:** plain text remains more reliable than required Unicode art:

```text
F.A.R.T. LAB :: UNIVERSAL EMISSION INSTRUMENT
MASS / MOMENTUM / ENERGY
```

## Color

| Token | Dark surface | Light surface | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Instrument | `#0B0F12` | `#0B0F12` | Structure and primary ink |
| Reference | `#F2EEE6` | `#F2EEE6` | Text, paper, reference geometry |
| Measurement | `#58D6D0` | `#006E6A` | Live flow and active measurement |
| Ledger | `#E6B85C` | `#7A5200` | Provenance, reference, uncertainty |
| Failure | `#F05D5E` | `#A33A3A` | Failed or impossible state only |

Color is never the only state carrier. Shape, label, position, and text remain
available. Ivory is not placed as small text on cyan or amber fills.

## Typography

The leading candidate family is IBM Plex Sans, Mono, and Math, distributed
under the SIL Open Font License. Noto or a reviewed script-specific family fills
language coverage gaps.

- The final wordmark is optically drawn and converted to vector outlines.
- Measurement tables use tabular figures.
- Units remain upright and subordinate to values.
- All caps are reserved for the name, state changes, and dry institutional
  declarations.
- Localized lockups receive native review and do not force Latin spacing rules
  onto other scripts.

## Motion

The Pressure Standard is the mark's motion grammar. A reference contour appears,
one event opens the boundary, mass, momentum, and energy respond, and a wavefront
crosses. During the vacuum interval, exterior acoustic geometry disappears while
structural response remains. The event decays. The Open Isobar holds.

Timing landmarks and ledger order stay recognizable. Microturbulence, decay
texture, and fresh-realization details may vary. Reduced-motion mode uses calm
state changes. Silent and captioned versions preserve the full account.

## Merchandise proof

The mark does not graduate from candidate status until it works as:

- A 16 px icon and a one-bit terminal mark.
- A one-color screen print, vinyl cut, and laser engraving.
- A 25 mm embroidered patch.
- An enamel form without fragile islands.
- A thermal-receipt and grayscale print.
- A masked macOS, Windows, Linux, and social icon.

Event-derived merchandise is the stronger long-term system: one-of-one
Plumeprint shirts, Fartflake sculptures, trace cards, ledger patches, and radio
station apparel that remains wearable without explaining the joke.

## Naming and legal gate

A preliminary search found other uses of “Fart Lab” in entertainment and apps.
The full expansion appears more distinctive, but that is not clearance. Before
expensive art, merchandise, store listings, domains, or trademark claims, a
qualified search must review the name, expansion, similar word marks, design
marks, common-law uses, and relevant software, game, education, media, and
merchandise classes in intended markets.

Apache License 2.0 covers repository source as declared. It does not grant broad
trademark rights. Official marks, community templates, event artifacts, music,
fonts, and third-party media therefore need explicit, separate policies. The
project will publish a permissive fan kit only after that boundary receives
legal review.

## Quality gate

- Review 16 through 1024 px, light, dark, one-color, one-bit, blur, grayscale,
  common color-vision simulations, and high-contrast themes.
- Test embroidery, screen print, engraving, vinyl, enamel, and icon masks.
- Test unprompted recognition and confusion with adjacent broken-ring marks.
- Perform name, design-code, and professional trademark clearance.
- Verify native-script lockups, alt text, reduced motion, and silent identity.
- Generate exports from version-controlled SVG and reject embedded rasters,
  unapproved fonts, metadata leakage, and output drift in CI.

The event happened once. The ledger remains.
