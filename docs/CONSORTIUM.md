# Intercontextual council prototype

This is a fictional, non-normative Broadcast and translation prototype. It is
not a transcript of a real institution, a claim about extraterrestrial life, or
an approved scientific standard. The English text is a player-facing rendering
of a hypothetical council record. It does not imply that every represented
participant uses symbols, language, voting, humor, or linear time.

The durable joke is institutional overconfidence:

> The instruments agree on a bounded relation. The council cannot agree what it
> means. Marketing has already printed UNIVERSAL TRANSLATOR on the box.

## Intercontextual Emission Interpretation Council

Working motto:

> Consensus on the retained claims. No consensus on what they meant.

Council roles are functions inside this authored presentation, not species or
nationalities:

- council chair;
- record clerk;
- law-context rapporteur;
- source-context advocate, only when a source participant and access basis exist;
- signal and channel analyst;
- structural-mapping reviewer;
- target-presentation or locale steward;
- accessibility reviewer;
- privacy and boundary reviewer;
- uncertainty auditor;
- comic-interpretation observer;
- marketing observer, whose overclaim is the recurring joke target.

The record preserves support, dissent, abstention, refusal, missing authority,
and multiple live hypotheses. It never compresses them into one translation
confidence percentage.

## Rendered discussion

**Record clerk:** Rendering notice. This transcript linearizes a council record
for an English-reading player. The source context supplies neither English nor a
total order. Apparent turn-taking belongs to this presentation.

**Law-context rapporteur:** The source pack defines a directed current and a
typed port relation. It defines no organism, object, anatomy, gas, sound,
language, intention, or source-law occurrence identity.

**Structural-mapping reviewer:** The proposed bridge preserves direction and one
normalized relation. It does not preserve material type, duration, geometry,
acoustics, or social meaning because the source context does not define them.

**Comic-interpretation observer:** So, structurally, a fart analogue?

**Law-context rapporteur:** Structurally, under one declared bridge, a partial
emission analogy. The word “fart” appears only in the player-facing comic view.

**Signal and channel analyst:** No source signal was segmented into symbols. We
have not translated a sentence, confession, warning, joke, or mating call.

**Privacy and boundary reviewer:** The absence of a privacy concept is not
consent. It is the absence of an applicable claim. Do not upgrade silence into
permission.

**Uncertainty auditor:** I object to the dashboard's 87 percent translation
confidence. Channel loss is not applicable to this structural mapping. Ontology
loss is high. Semantic confidence is not defined. One number conceals the
failure.

**Marketing observer:** The packaging is already in transit.

**Target-locale steward:** The localized joke may call this a fart analogue if
the locale review supports it. The archive keeps the stable structural result
and the limits. No locale may change the law claim.

**Council chair:** Disposition: retain the structural mapping, refuse semantic
equivalence, label the comic gloss, publish the minority report, and request a
larger sticker for “UNDER FORMAL PROTEST.”

**Minority report:** The phrase “fart analogue” is useful as a disclosed comic
projection, but it is not a translation. Publishing it without the adjacent
limits would falsely imply a source occurrence, body, intention, and shared
meaning that this case does not supply.

## Product rules demonstrated

This prototype is useful only if it becomes an inspectable product contract:

1. Law-supported claims appear before interpretation.
2. Physical or structural classification is distinct from semantic translation.
3. A localized gloss is stored as presentation, never canonical source meaning.
4. Loss is a typed vector, not one confidence scalar.
5. Multiple hypotheses, dissent, abstention, privacy, and refusal survive export.
6. Presentation ordering cannot become source-law time.
7. The joke targets the Lab's paperwork and marketing, not a culture, species,
   body, disability, sanitation system, language, or belief.
