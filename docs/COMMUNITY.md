# Community and meme culture

F.A.R.T. Lab should not manufacture memes. It should manufacture meaningful
scientific objects that are funny enough for people to turn into memes.

The culture loop is:

1. A Lab encounter receives a unique record identity.
2. The instrument retains only the trace its recording policy permits.
3. The trace becomes a shareable artifact.
4. Someone remixes it without disguising its scientific status.
5. The community invents language around it.
6. The best community language may eventually enter the instrument.

If removing a joke would also remove scientific information, the joke probably
belongs in the product.

| `TRACE RETAINED` | Evidence remains, not the encounter |
| The ledger balances | Conservation checks passed |
| Conservation of Ass | Conserved transfers have not vanished from the model |
| `BOUNDARY CROSSED` | A calculated classification transition occurred |
| Not a numerical artifact | A result survived the declared refinement check |
| Same Pi, different sky | Similarity under different sources, scales, or worlds |
| External acoustics unavailable | The exterior has no supported sound channel |
| Banal by default | The reference event is intentionally ordinary |

Official copy introduces phrases slowly. Community language is allowed to be
better than planned copy.

## Remix primitives

The CLI will eventually export local, open templates rather than a social feed:

- Plumeprint contour and vortex skeleton.
- Pressure history and acoustic spectrogram strips.
- Active dimensionless signature.
- Mass circle, momentum chevron, and energy diamond.
- Boundary-transition and convergence stamps.
- Applicable uncertainty band and measurement or view marker.
- Record-lineage and status plates. `EVENT PASSED` appears only when the source
  ordering supports that claim.
- Flat reference code and Fartflake verification view.

Evidence views keep units and provenance. Once a remix changes quantitative
encoding it says `REMIXED TRACE`. No remix can present itself as an official
verification certificate.

## Share cards

- **Occurrence Card:** scope, law contexts, provenance, and supported relations.
- **Witness Card:** what was measured, plus location, ordering, and uncertainty
  only when those concepts exist.
- **Ledger Card:** applicable residuals, implementation, refinement, and claim state.
- **Boundary Card:** a transition, causal values, and crossing time only for a
  profile that defines all three.
- **Postcard From Elsewhere:** any supported setting, source, observer, and
  clearly separated lore and computed claims.

Fields are capability-selected, so an absent source, observer, location, time,
solver, unit, or uncertainty never becomes a blank or fake zero. Every card
includes meaningful alt text, a short record identifier, schema and applicable
implementation versions, a visible `SIMULATED OCCURRENCE` label, and no default personal
or tool byline. Local export is the default. The complete game does not require
an account, public gallery, or web application.

Share tools default to fictional entity handles and strip names, account
handles, precise location, machine data, archive secrets, and unrelated
metadata. Agent exports remain local until a person deliberately publishes
them. Publication never happens as a side effect of generation or play.

## Community rituals

- **The Standard Pfft:** each release begins with the banal reference fixture
  and reports meaningful changes.
- **Grid Refinement Friday:** demonstrate whether an absurd result survives a
  stricter timestep or grid.
- **Same Pi, Different Sky:** reproduce a dimensionless behavior in a different
  being, scale, medium, or universe.
- **Boundary Review:** conduct a mock evidence-based hearing on a disputed
  wetness, choking, plasma, or classification transition.
- **The Vanishing Session:** attend one Lab encounter without a full recording.
- **Postcards From Elsewhere:** contribute a nonhuman or alternate-law viewpoint
  that passes science and cultural review.
- **Radio Crate Drop:** release a small station pack whose music is good before
  its lore is noticed.

The first public release should launch only The Standard Pfft, Grid Refinement
Friday, and Same Pi, Different Sky. Rituals survive because people enjoy them,
not because a content calendar requires them.

## Fan culture boundaries

The joke targets physical systems, bureaucracy, impossible precision, and the
confidence of the instrument. It does not target people.

- No bodily stereotype tied to nationality, race, ethnicity, caste, religion,
  poverty, disability, gender, sexuality, body type, illness, or sanitation.
- Locale jokes come from language structure, sound symbolism, register,
  euphemism, typography, translation failure, and bureaucracy, not people.
- Alternate beings do not act as disguised real-world groups.
- Default visuals remain non-graphic.
- Agents cannot mass-post, impersonate consensus, target users, or manufacture
  social proof.
- No nonconsensual attribution of a simulated bodily event to an identifiable
  person, group, religion, disability, sanitation condition, or impoverished
  place. Doxxing, voice impersonation, bodily accusations, stereotype-coded
  remixes, and fake verification claims are prohibited.
- Official community spaces require private reporting, documented enforcement,
  appeals, and at least two human contacts before launch. Reporting and appeals
  cover remixes as well as official posts.

Japanese ideas related to impermanence can inform the design, but they are not
merch slogans or mystical decoration. French and Japanese material requires
named reviewers competent in the exact language, script, region, humor, and
cultural context. National identity alone neither qualifies nor disqualifies a
reviewer.

## Contributor paths

People can contribute without writing simulation code:

- **Field Note:** scientific, design, linguistic, or cultural observation.
- **Specimen:** reproducible trace or regression case.
- **Instrument Patch:** code, solver, CLI, TUI, or visualization work.
- **Boundary Proposal:** a testable new classification.
- **Broadcast Contribution:** radio, music, lore, or audio production.
- **Translation Note:** reviewed terminology, wordplay, or locale behavior.
- **Culture Review:** contextual assessment of a scenario or joke.
- **Merch Proof:** physical-production test of a brand asset.
- **Verification Report:** invariant, convergence, fuzz, or platform evidence.

The project recognizes contributors in history, release notes, optional
profiles, and asset metadata where requested. The Occurrence Card itself remains
about the occurrence record. Required third-party notices remain available even
though the product carries no visible creator or tool attribution.

## What success means

Do not optimize only for impressions. Measure the portion of shared work derived
from actual state, remixes versus untouched templates, new contributor and
reviewer retention, native-review completion, alt-text and privacy-lint coverage,
moderation response, and the number of claims linked to executable evidence.

The desired feeling is a scientific community accidentally becoming funny, not
a brand account trying to go viral.
