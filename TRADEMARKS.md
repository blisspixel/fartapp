`F.A.R.T. Lab`, `Flatulence Aerodynamics Research & Testing`, the Open Isobar

identifiers. They have not completed professional clearance and are not
represented here as registered marks.

Until a reviewed fan policy is published:

- Truthful reference to the project, unmodified screenshots, commentary,
  criticism, and uses allowed by applicable law remain unaffected.
- Do not imply that a fork, product, account, event, certificate, or item is
  official, verified, sponsored, or endorsed.
- Do not use the candidate official mark as another application's icon or
  remove scientific failure status from an altered certificate.
- Do not describe the Pressure Standard as government, medical, safety,
  environmental, or third-party certification.

The planned Community Kit will use a visibly different community mark and
low-friction asset license so people can make memes, videos, art, and unofficial
goods without confusing them with official releases. Its policy will not limit
parody, criticism, commentary, or other rights provided by law.

A comprehensive word, common-law, app-store, domain, international, and design
search plus qualified legal review is required before final naming, mark lock,
store launch, or trademark filing. This file is project status and design intent,
not legal advice.
