# Community conduct

F.A.R.T. Lab welcomes thoughtful, curious, and good-humored participation.

Participants are expected to:

- Treat people with respect across identity, background, experience, ability,
  and communication style.
- Critique ideas, models, code, and evidence without attacking people.
- Keep vulgar comedy focused on the absurd premise, institutional pomposity, and
  fictional situations rather than real demographics or cultures.
- Respect accessibility needs, content controls, and personal boundaries.
- Avoid harassment, threats, discrimination, sexual targeting, doxxing, spam,
  and deliberate disruption.
- Handle security reports and personal information privately and responsibly.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when
conduct undermines a safe, constructive project. Serious conduct or safety
concerns should be reported privately to the repository owner through GitHub.
